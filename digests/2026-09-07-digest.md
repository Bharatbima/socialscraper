## 🏛️ THEME 1: IRDAI's Public Insurance Registry (PIR) — The Big Story This Week

> *Multiple sources covered this; consolidated into one entry.*

**Summary:** IRDAI has launched a public consultation on a proposed Public Insurance Registry (PIR) — a centralised digital infrastructure that would link policy data, claims history, and risk profiles across all insurers, and connect with identity systems, lenders, and public databases. Policyholders would be able to view all their policies in one place and make common service requests; insurers would gain access to anonymised industry-wide claims data; intermediaries and regulators would benefit from reduced information asymmetry. IRDAI says the PIR will boost transparency, reduce fraud, widen coverage, and enable low-cost insurance purchases.

**Why it matters for brokers:** PIR could fundamentally change how group health and life policies are serviced, how claims are verified, and how brokers demonstrate value-add to clients. For MFI-linked insurance, it could reduce duplicate coverage and improve enrolment accuracy. Brokers should engage with the consultation process.
